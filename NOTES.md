This repo predicts whether an MLB team covers the run line, not who wins the game.

Completed games were pulled from the MLB Stats API. A home cover is labeled when home score minus away score is greater than 1.5.

Minimum date: 2023-03-30
Maximum date: 2026-10-28
Row count: 9889

mlb_game_predictor features and predictions were not copied. The schedule was checked through 2026-11-07. This does not price a bet.
